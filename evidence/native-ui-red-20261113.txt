命令（旧提交 worktree，不影响主仓库）：

  cp tools/native_ui/RLPFunctionalAudit.mm /tmp/rlp-old/tools/native_ui/

  cmake --build /tmp/rlp-old/build-mac --target rlplayer_native -j8
